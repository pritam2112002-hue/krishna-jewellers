OUR STORY

A Jewellery Legacy Built Across Generations

Our family tradition in jewellery goes back generations, beginning with Late Shri Hansraj Soni and carried forward by Shri Omprakash Soni, who has been actively building the family jewellery business for more than 26 years. Krishna Jewellers was established in 2021. Today, Preetam Soni carries this tradition forward into its third generation.
